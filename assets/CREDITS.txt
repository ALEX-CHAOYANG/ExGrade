ExGrade portal artwork: sources, licences and changes

Body maps: body/human.svg, body/mouse.svg, body/rat.svg
  Adapted from the EMBL-EBI Expression Atlas anatomograms homo_sapiens.male.svg, mus_musculus.male.svg and rattus_norvegicus.svg
  (npm package @ebi-gene-expression-group/anatomogram 2.4.0; https://github.com/ebi-gene-expression-group/anatomogram).
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0), https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/
  Changes: organs the portal does not use were removed and the rest grouped into the portal's tissues and recoloured;
  shapes were drawn in for organs the anatomograms do not show: supraclavicular brown adipose tissue (human); hippocampus,
  gonadal white adipose tissue and pancreas (mouse); hippocampus, spinal cord, aorta, interscapular brown and retroperitoneal
  white adipose tissue, pancreas, thigh muscle, forelimb bones with marrow, and elbow cartilage (rat).
